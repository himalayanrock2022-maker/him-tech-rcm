WHO WE ARE

About Hima Tech RCM

Hima Tech RCM is a medical billing and revenue cycle management company providing services from Pakistan to healthcare providers in the United States.

We support independent physicians, medical practices, clinics, and healthcare organizations with practical revenue cycle solutions designed around their operational needs.

Our services cover the revenue cycle, from provider credentialing and payer enrollment to claims management, payment posting, accounts receivable follow-up, and denial management.

We believe healthcare providers should be able to focus on patient care while having a dependable team supporting their billing and administrative operations.
